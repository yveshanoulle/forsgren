DORA bands of deployment frequency, from the count of the last 30 days: on demand (multiple deploys per day) 720 and more, between once per hour and once per day 30 to 719, between once per day and once per week 5 to 29, between once per week and once per month 1 to 4; with none in the last 30 days, from the count of the last 180 days: between once per month and once every six months 1 and more, less than once per six months 0.

DORA bands of lead time for changes, from the median of the commits deployed in the last 30 days: less than one hour below 1 hour, less than one day 1 hour to below 24 hours, one day to one week 24 hours to below 7 days, one week to one month 7 days to below 30 days, one to six months 30 days to below 180 days, more than six months 180 days and more. A month is 30 days.

A failure not recovered yet is counted, not timed.
